Nevada · Clark County

Business License Steps

A practical preparation checklist based on the Clark County Department of Business License application checklist dated August 13, 2026. Start by checking jurisdiction; rules differ inside incorporated cities.

Check Jurisdiction ↗Official County Checklist (PDF) ↗
Important: This guide summarizes Clark County's published checklist. It is not an application or an eligibility decision. Verify your address, business type, current fees, and permits with the responsible agency before submitting.

Prepare before applying

Use the checklist below to track your preparation. Checkmarks are for this visit only and aren't submitted or stored.

1

Confirm your jurisdiction and zoning

Check the business address in the county's Jurisdiction Lookup. The Clark County checklist applies to unincorporated Clark County; confirm permitted activities and zoning before you apply. Planning: (702) 455-4314.

2

Obtain a Nevada State Business License

Register the entity, if applicable, with the Nevada Secretary of State and obtain the appropriate State Business License. Start at Nevada SilverFlume.

3

Register with the Nevada Department of Taxation

Complete the applicable state tax registrations, including through SilverFlume.

4

Register a fictitious business name (DBA), if needed

If the business uses a name different from the owner's legal name or registered entity name, file a Fictitious Firm Name certificate with the Clark County Clerk. Match the entity type shown in state records.

5

Document your physical business location

Prepare evidence of your right to use the location and the landlord/lessor information requested on the application. The county says mailboxes and P.O. boxes do not qualify as physical business locations.

6

Identify the license category and fees

The county checklist lists a $45 nonrefundable application fee, plus the applicable business license fee, which varies by activity. Multiple business activities may require multiple licenses. Verify the current fee and NAICS category before paying.

7

Obtain a health permit if your business requires one

Businesses such as food establishments and public pools/spas may need proof of application to the Southern Nevada Health District. Check industry-specific requirements.

8

Obtain professional board approval if applicable

Businesses regulated by state boards—such as construction, medical, cosmetology, real estate, and other regulated fields—may need professional licenses or certificates.

9

Complete and submit the county application

After satisfying the prerequisites, open the Clark County application portal. The county checklist says applications should be submitted within 30 days of opening and construction/tenant improvements must be complete.

Source: Clark County Department of Business License — Application Checklist, 08/13/2026. For county questions: (702) 455-4340.
