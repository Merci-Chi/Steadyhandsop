# Mikes Quality Dent Repair — Luxury Animated Build

Open `index.html`. All 12 self-contained generated PNG assets are inside `assets/overlays/` and used across the site. Scroll animations and cursor settings live in `assets/luxury-motion.css` and `assets/luxury-motion.js`.

Cursor: desktop fine-pointer only; white hollow circle with `mix-blend-mode:difference` appears black over white and white over black. Hovering links/buttons creates a small solid inverted dot. The default cursor returns on touch devices and if JavaScript cannot run.

Motion: section and individual content reveals use IntersectionObserver once, do not repeat when scrolling back. Reduced-motion system preference is honored.

Icons: inline SVG line icons use the open-source Lucide visual vocabulary (https://lucide.dev/icons/ and https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide, ISC license); no emoji or font/text character icons remain. SVGs are included locally so they do not depend on icon CDNs.

The business photos remain illustrative stock imagery and do not depict the company. Existing photo links may require internet access. Before publishing confirm address, phone, reviews and photo licensing. All copy preserves company name.
