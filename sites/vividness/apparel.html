What apparel can I personalize?

The current range includes shirts, sweatshirts, hoodies, polos, hats, and business apparel. Contact us to discuss garment options, colors, sizes, and availability.

Can I order for a group or business?

Yes. Vivid works with individuals, families, businesses, and organizations. Include your approximate quantity and sizing needs when you request a quote.

How do I get pricing?

Tell us your item type, quantity, colors, artwork or wording, and deadline. Pricing and ordering are confirmed directly with the team.
