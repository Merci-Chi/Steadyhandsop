 Pocono Villa Resort by Exploria

Escape to the scenic beauty of the Pocono Mountains in Pennsylvania with our exclusive vacation package! Relax in a spacious two-bedroom villa at Pocono Villa Resort by Exploria and enjoy both nature and family-friendly activities right at your doorstep.

 Two-Bedroom Villa – Sleeps Up to 6 Guests 

Spacious and comfortable, perfect for families or small groups. Package includes a $100 Mastercard

Layout & Features:

  • Bedroom 1: King bed, flat-screen TV, and private bathroom

  • Bedroom 2: Two double beds, flat-screen TV

  • Bathrooms: Two full bathrooms (one in master, one shared)

  • Living Area: Queen sleeper sofa, fireplace, flat-screen TV, DVD player

  • Kitchen: Fully equipped with refrigerator, stove/oven, microwave, dishwasher, cookware & utensils

  • Dining Area: Seating for six

  • Laundry: In-unit washer & dryer

  • Outdoor Space: Private balcony or patio with scenic mountain or wooded views

  • Extras: Complimentary Wi-Fi, central heating & air conditioning, coffee maker, iron & ironing board, hairdryer

 On-Site Resort Amenities – Pocono Villa Resort

  • Indoor swimming pool & heated outdoor pool (seasonal)

  • Hot tub and sauna

  • Fitness center

  • Mini-golf course & tennis courts

  • Playground & game room

  • Picnic and BBQ areas

  • Walking trails through the resort grounds

  • Organized activities for kids and adults

  • On-site restaurant & lounge

 Things to Do Around the Pocono Mountains

Outdoor Adventures

  • Bushkill Falls – “The Niagara of Pennsylvania,” with hiking trails and scenic waterfalls

  • Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area – kayaking, canoeing, fishing, and hiking

  • Camelback Mountain Resort – skiing, snowboarding, and snow tubing in winter; waterpark and zipline in summer

  • Big Pocono State Park – breathtaking panoramic views and hiking trails

Family Fun & Entertainment

  • Great Wolf Lodge – indoor water park fun

  • Kalahari Resorts – America’s largest indoor water park

  • Shawnee Mountain Ski Area – seasonal skiing and snowboarding

  • Pocono Raceway – NASCAR races and motorsports events

Shopping & Local Flavor

  • The Crossings Premium Outlets – 100+ brand-name stores

  • Local wineries & breweries – wine tastings and tours

  • Quaint small towns with antique shops and farm markets
