  • In Williamsburg, VA, you can immerse yourself in the 18th century at the living-history museum of Colonial Williamsburg, explore the original site of the first English settlement at Historic Jamestowne, or experience the replica settlement at Jamestown Settlement. For thrill-seekers, Busch Gardens Williamsburg offers roller coasters, while Water Country USA provides water park fun. You can also visit the Governor’s Palace, the Art Museums of Colonial Williamsburg, or take a Williamsburg ghost tour. 

     Historical & Cultural Experiences
      • Colonial Williamsburg:

        .

        Step back in time at this massive living-history museum, which features costumed interpreters, restored buildings like the Governor’s Palace and the Capitol, and trade shops. 

         
    • Historic Jamestowne:

      .

      Visit the actual archaeological site where the original 1607 English settlers established their settlement. 

       
    • Jamestown Settlement:

      .

      Explore a re-creation of the 1607 settlement, complete with replica ships and life-size models of the settlers’ and American Indians’ homes. 

       
    • Art Museums of Colonial Williamsburg:

      .

      Discover the folk and decorative art collections at these free museums within the Colonial Williamsburg area. 

       
    Amusement & Family Fun
    • Busch Gardens Williamsburg:

      .

      Enjoy a day at this award-winning theme park, known for its world-class roller coasters and beautiful grounds. 

       
    • Water Country USA:

      .

      Cool off at this family-friendly water park, featuring slides, a lazy river, and a wave pool. 

       
    Other Activities
    • Williamsburg ghost tour: Take a spooky walk through the historic town to learn about its darker past. 
       
    • Explore the city: Visit the campus of the College of William & Mary, one of the nation’s premier public universities. 
       
    • Take the Jamestown-Scotland Ferry: Enjoy a scenic and historic ferry ride across the James River.
