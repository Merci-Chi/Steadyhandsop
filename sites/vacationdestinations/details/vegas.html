Las Vegas Getaway! 

West Gate  Resort

During your trip, sample fare from top chefs and delicious buffets, try your luck at one of the world’s premier casinos, or take in a spectacular show. Just wandering the Las Vegas Strip is enough to get your heart pumping. Once you’ve had enough of the razzle-dazzle, wave hello to the sea life at the Mandalay Bay Shark Reef, hike nearby Red Rock Canyon, or visit the Neon Boneyard, where old signs take on a new life.
