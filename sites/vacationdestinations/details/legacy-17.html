  • This lakefront resort in Lake Havasu City, Arizona features a 9-hole golf course, on-site dining and a splash pool with a waterslide. It also offers spacious suites with a kitchenette. All suites offer private bedrooms and a separate living room with a dining table. Cable TVs, free Wi-Fi are included. Each suite is individually decorated. A marina and boat dock are on site at London Bridge Resort. Boat rentals, including jet skis, paddle boats and canoes, are also available. Guests can relax in the 3 outdoor pools or work out in the gym. Resort London Bridge’s Lake Havasu Spa has private treatment rooms for massages. Martini Bay restaurant, which overlooks the London Bridge, serves signature chef dishes. Kokomo Pool Bar and Nightclub is adjacent to the resort. Lake Havasu State Park is 2.1 mi from this resort. while London Bridge is a 5-minute walk away.

    Couples in particular like the location – they rated it 9.3 for a two-person trip.
