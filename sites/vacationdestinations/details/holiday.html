We had a great time and really liked the hotel. It was a near perfect experience at the hotel. Checking in was a breeze, the room was big and very clean. The reason why I said it was a near perfect experience was b’cuz, 1, the safe in the room did not work and we were very skeptical about leaving any valuables in the room. And 2, the electrical outlet in the bathroom did not work either prohibiting my wife from blow drying her hair in the bathroom. Other than that everything was great. I would definitely stay there again should we need to.
