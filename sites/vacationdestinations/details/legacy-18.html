The resort

Relax, splash and stay close to Orlando’s biggest attractions

Westgate Lakes Resort & Spa is a full-service lakefront resort at 9500 Turkey Lake Road in Orlando. Guests can enjoy resort pools and hot tubs, onsite dining, recreational activities and convenient access to the theme parks.
