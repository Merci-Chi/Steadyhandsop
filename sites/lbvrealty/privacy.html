YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This page explains how information may be handled when you use this website or contact La Belle Vie Property Management, LLC.

Information you choose to share

If you call or email us, you may provide contact details and information about your rental inquiry. We use information you provide to respond to your request and communicate about properties or services.

Links to other services

Property applications and inquiries may take you to a third-party property management portal. Our social media links take you to Facebook or Instagram. Those services have their own privacy practices and policies; please review them before submitting personal information.

Website and technical information

This is an informational website. Depending on how it is hosted and configured, the hosting provider may process standard technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, and access logs for delivery, operations, or security. Any additional analytics, cookies, or tracking should be separately disclosed if enabled.

Information protection and retention

We aim to handle information you send us responsibly and retain it only as needed for the relevant business purpose and applicable requirements. Please avoid emailing highly sensitive personal or financial information; use the designated secure application portal when appropriate.

Questions or privacy requests

For questions about personal information you shared directly with us, please contact Hello@lbvpm.com or (910) 691-5556.
