Before your visit

A few things to know.

Can you create a custom piece?

Yes. Anka offers one-of-a-kind custom designs, including engagement rings. Call us to discuss your ideas, preferred gemstones and budget.

Can you resize or repair my jewelry?

We offer jewelry repairs, ring resizing, cleaning and polishing. Call or bring your piece to the store to discuss the work it needs.

Do you service watches?

For watch battery replacement or repairs, call us with your watch's brand and the issue so we can confirm whether we can help.

How much will it cost?

Contact the store about your specific repair or custom design. Ask for an estimate after discussing the piece, materials and work involved.

How long will it take?

Timing depends on the piece and the work required. Tell us if you have a special date in mind, and call to confirm an estimated completion time.

Do I need an appointment?

Please call (702) 365-8663 before visiting to confirm current hours and arrange a time to discuss your piece.
