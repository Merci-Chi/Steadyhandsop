AUTOMATIC FORWARD / REVERSE HERO FILM
The top banner uses your supplied watch-and-ring recording in a silent,
automatic loop. The middle plays at the original recording speed. The final stretch
gradually decelerates for 0.8 seconds before each reversal, then accelerates
for 0.8 seconds into the opposite direction. There is no hold.
Motion-compensated interpolation supplies extra frames for smooth slow motion.
The forward/reverse sequence is encoded into the video for native playback,
without repeated seeking or negative playback rates.

Scrolling and mouse movement do not control the film.
Playback continues when the banner scrolls offscreen.
There is no pinned section, extra scrolling distance or progress bar.
A small Pause film / Play film button is provided in the top banner.
Playback pauses when the browser tab is hidden.
Reduced Motion starts with a still banner; the play button can enable it.
Autoplay restrictions fall back to the Play film button.

Files:
images/hero-loop.mp4 — silent, approximately 9.57-second forward/reverse H.264 loop.
Each direction lasts approximately 4.78 seconds, including the gradual end easing;
the empty tail and duplicate endpoint frames are omitted so reversals do not pause.
images/hero-poster.webp — still frame while loading or when playback is off.
The other photos, ratings, source links and contact section remain included.
