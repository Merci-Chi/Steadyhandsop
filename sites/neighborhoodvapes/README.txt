Icons: Lucide SVG shapes (ISC License), https://lucide.dev/license ; embedded locally for offline reliability.
Scroll effects: IntersectionObserver, staggered, once per section; reduced-motion accessibility fallback.
Custom cursor: white outlined circle; shrinks to filled dot over clickables. CSS difference blending makes it black over light surfaces and white over dark surfaces. Disabled on touch and for users who prefer reduced motion. Embedded Google Maps owns its cursor while hovered.
