The website lists tumblers, drinkware, wood gifts, and keepsakes. Item and material suitability must be confirmed directly with the team.

Can I use my own artwork or wording?

Vivid offers names, messages, logos, and custom artwork. Share your design idea so the team can discuss how it will work on your chosen item.

Can I bring an item to be engraved?

Ask the team before supplying an item. They can confirm whether the material, size, and design are suitable.
